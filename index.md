# Qevorlix Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026. This policy applies to the Qevorlix Pin Publisher app connected to the Pinterest API.

## What this app is

A personal tool run by a single Pinterest account owner (Qevorlix). It publishes the owner's own product videos as Pins to the owner's own boards and reads the owner's own boards. It is not offered to other users.

## Data the app accesses

The owner's board names and IDs, the Pins and videos the app itself creates, and public keyword trend data from the Pinterest API. It does not access other people's Pins, boards, profiles, followers or messages.

## How data is used and stored

Only to publish and organize the owner's Pins. Data is processed on the owner's own computer. The access token and a list of already-posted products are stored only on that computer.

## Sharing

No Pinterest data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Pins link to Shopee product pages through affiliate links, and every Pin description discloses the affiliate link.

## Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings and delete the local files. Pins published by the app can be deleted on Pinterest like any other Pin.
